## Audit conclusions

- Every candidate now has a verified paper/release identity except the
  SEARCHLIGHT download endpoint, which is explicitly recorded as unavailable
  in the inspected public paper.
- No candidate is approved as a directly replayable enterprise workload.
  Conditional roles are limited to the distributions stated above.
- No raw records from different sources may be concatenated into a claimed
  enterprise trace.
- The 40 GB CESNET-TimeSeries24 full-IP archive, the 750 GB SEARCHLIGHT data,
  and the compressed CTU-SME-11/Real Network PCAP archives were not downloaded
  locally. This audit records remote file evidence, not a completed
  distribution extraction.
- A v1/v2 schema comparison for SDNCampus and owner-approved access to
  SEARCHLIGHT remain follow-up tasks before either can be used beyond the
  stated conditional role.
